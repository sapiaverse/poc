About these records

A replay of the 50 synthetic residents of EXP-001 over 1–21 April 2024, read from saved logs. Pick a resident, pick a day, then play or drag along their day.

What you are looking at

  • Playback reads saved logs. It makes no model calls and does not rerun CitySim.
  • Locations, lives and relationships are simulation assumptions, not observed human histories.
  • Trip lines are straight segments between logged departure and arrival points, not GPS traces or street routes.
  • Reflections and reasons are explicit model outputs, not hidden internal reasoning. Plans describe intent; actions describe what was executed.
  • Gaps between activity records stay unknown; the map then shows the last known location as a hollow dot. Events are assigned to residents only when their recorded scope names them.
  • Records after the playhead are faded. “Hide later records” removes them.
  • Map colours group the 19 native activity labels into five classes for legibility; the record cards keep the original label.
  • The saved needs log keeps only the first 40 characters of each emotion. About a third reach that limit; they end in “…” here.
